WHITEBOARD STUDIO — Essay to hand-drawn video
=============================================

WINDOWS: Double-click start.bat.
The app opens at http://localhost:4178

1. Paste your essay.
2. Click "Create my storyboard".
3. Press Play to preview, and edit any scenes you want.
4. Optional: "Generate Windows voice" or "Import audio".
5. "Export MP4" or "Export WebM".

MP4 requires free FFmpeg. If the app says FFmpeg is missing,
double-click install-ffmpeg.bat and restart the app.
WebM export does not need FFmpeg.

Requires Node.js 18+ (your laptop already has Node installed).
No npm install. No external API keys. No login.
Ollama AI storyboard is optional; instant scenes work offline.

Keep the browser tab visible while exporting a video.
Long videos record in real time.

For help and details, see README.md.
